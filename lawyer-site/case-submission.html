    What happens next

    1. The advocate reads your submission personally, usually the same day.
    2. A conflict check is run against the other side’s name.
    3. You receive a written assessment within two working days: the legal position, your options, a likely timeline and an estimate of cost.
    4. If you wish to proceed, a consultation is arranged and an engagement letter issued.

    If it cannot wait

    An arrest, an eviction tomorrow morning, or a hearing this week is not a form matter. Telephone the chamber directly.

    +880 1711-234567 WhatsApp

    Writing a useful summary

    • Start at the beginning, not at the part that upset you most.
    • Give dates, even approximate ones.
    • Name every person and company involved.
    • Say what the other side claims, not only your side.
    • Mention any deadline printed on a notice you received.
