Before you come

Make the visit count

Yes, for the Supreme Court room always. At Dhanmondi, walk-ins are seen when the advocate is free, but telephoning first spares you a wasted journey — court sittings often run late.

Your NID or passport, and every paper connected to the matter — deeds, notices, court orders, receipts, messages. Bring originals to be seen, and photocopies to be left behind.

A relative may come for a first discussion, but the advocate can only take instructions from you. For clients abroad, a registered power of attorney is arranged through the nearest Bangladesh mission.

The written assessment that follows an online case submission is free. A consultation at the chamber is BDT 2,000–5,000 depending on length, and is credited against your fee if you go on to engage the chamber.

Tell the chamber what happened

Ten minutes on the form, a written answer in two working days, and no fee until you decide to proceed.
