Why clients stay

Most people leave a lawyer’s office more confused than when they walked in.

That is the problem this chamber was built to solve. Every matter begins with a written assessment in ordinary Bangla or English: what the law says, what it will realistically cost, how long it will take, and what could go wrong.

You will always know which stage your case is at, because you receive an update after every hearing — not only when a fee is due.

More about the advocate

Fees agreed in writing, in advance

A signed engagement letter states the professional fee, court costs and what is excluded. No invoice arrives that you have not already seen.

One advocate, start to finish

The person who hears your story is the person who argues it. Junior counsel assist with filing, never with strategy.

An honest answer about your chances

Where a settlement serves you better than a decade of litigation, that is what you will be advised — even though it earns the chamber less.

How it works

From your first message to a filed case

Four steps. You know exactly what happens next at every point.

Send the facts

Fill in the case form with dates, parties and documents. Ten minutes of your time replaces a wasted trip to the chamber.

Within two working days you receive a note on the legal position, options, likely timeline and an estimate of costs.

Consultation
