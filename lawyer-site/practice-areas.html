What this chamber actually does

Eight areas of settled practice, each with the courts involved, the documents you should gather, and an honest note on how long it usually takes.

Family & Matrimonial

Divorce and khula, recovery of unpaid dower (den mohor) and maintenance, custody and guardianship, restitution of conjugal rights, and enforcement of family court decrees that the other side is ignoring.

Forum: Family Court; appeals to the District Judge
Bring: Nikahnama, talaq notice, NID copies, proof of payments
Typical time: 8–20 months

  • Dower & maintenance
  • Custody
  • Guardianship

Land & Property

Title verification before you buy, mutation (namjari) and record-of-rights correction, partition among heirs, declaration and permanent injunction suits, and disputes with developers over handover and delay.

Forum: Assistant Judge / Joint District Judge; AC (Land) office
Bring: Deed, khatian, mutation order, DCR, rent receipts, survey map
Typical time: 1–4 years for a contested suit

  • Title search
  • Mutation
  • Partition

Criminal Defence

Anticipatory and regular bail, quashment of a malicious proceeding, cheque dishonour cases under section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, and defence at trial before the Magistrate and Sessions courts.

Forum: Magistrate, Sessions Judge; High Court Division for bail and quashment
Bring: FIR or petition case copy, charge sheet, previous bail orders
Typical time: Bail in days; trial 1–3 years

  • Bail
  • Quashment
  • NI Act 138

Company & Commercial

Company and partnership formation, RJSC filings and annual returns, shareholder and director disputes, drafting and review of contracts, joint venture and distribution agreements, and voluntary winding up.

Forum: RJSC; Company Bench of the High Court Division
Bring: Memorandum & articles, incorporation certificate, board minutes, the contract in dispute
Typical time: Formation 2–4 weeks; disputes vary

  • RJSC
  • Shareholder disputes
  • Contracts

Labour & Employment

Claims for wrongful termination, unpaid wages, gratuity and provident fund, service benefits on retirement, labour court proceedings under the Labour Act 2006, and settlement negotiation for employers.

Forum: Labour Court; Labour Appellate Tribunal
Bring: Appointment letter, pay slips, termination or show-cause letter, service book
Typical time: 10–24 months

  • Termination
  • Unpaid wages
  • Compliance

Writ & Constitutional

Petitions under Article 102 of the Constitution where a public authority has acted without lawful authority: licence cancellation, service and promotion matters, demolition or eviction notices, and refusal to decide an application at all.

Forum: High Court Division; appeal to the Appellate Division
Bring: The impugned order or notice, your representation and any reply, proof of service
Typical time: Rule issued in weeks; final hearing 1–3 years

  • Article 102
  • Service matters
  • Natural justice

Banking & Money Loan Court

Defence and negotiation in Artha Rin (money loan) suits, restructuring and settlement with lenders, release of mortgaged property, and challenges to auction and possession notices.

Forum: Artha Rin Adalat; High Court Division on revision
Bring: Loan agreement, mortgage deed, statement of account, bank notices
Typical time: 1–2 years; settlements faster

  • Artha Rin
  • Mortgage release
  • Settlement

Non-Resident Bangladeshi Matters

Acting for clients who cannot attend court in person: drafting and attestation of powers of attorney at Bangladesh missions abroad, protecting inherited land, succession certificates, and remote handling of family suits.

Forum: Any competent court, acting under your power of attorney
Bring: Passport copy, proof of relationship, property papers, mission appointment
Typical time: Power of attorney 2–5 weeks including attestation

  • Power of attorney
  • Succession
  • Remote hearings

Fees

What it is likely to cost

Indicative ranges only. Your engagement letter states the exact figure before any work begins, and court fees are always charged at actual cost against receipts.

Consultation

Free first assessment

The written assessment that follows your case submission costs nothing. A follow-up consultation at the chamber is BDT 2,000–5,000, credited against your fee if you engage.

Fixed fee per stage

Most matters

Drafting, filing and hearings are quoted separately, so you pay as the case advances rather than in one lump at the start. Instalments are available in family and labour matters.

Retainer

Businesses

A monthly retainer covering contract review, notices, compliance filings and unlimited telephone advice, with litigation quoted separately when it arises.

Be cautious of any lawyer who guarantees a result or asks for a large cash payment without a receipt. This chamber issues a receipt for every taka received and puts every fee in writing.

Tell the chamber what happened

Ten minutes on the form, a written answer in two working days, and no fee until you decide to proceed.
